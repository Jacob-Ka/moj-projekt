How it works

1

Connect your store

Connect Shopify or WooCommerce, or upload a CSV. Products are matched across shops by UPC / EAN / GTIN. Set a minimum price for every product.

2

We watch the market

We check Google Shopping (US, UK, Germany and more) and the competitor product pages you add, and show the lowest, median and highest price.

3

You stay in control

Get a suggested price with the reasoning. Approve it in one click, or let a rule apply it, for example "$0.50 below the cheapest competitor" or "1% below". Every change is logged.
