Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026
This document is a starter draft prepared as a reference point, not legal advice. We recommend having it reviewed by a privacy lawyer before processing customer data at scale.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The controller of personal data processed in connection with PriceAI Cloud is Jacob Kaczmarczyk, an individual based in Poland who does not currently operate a registered business. Contact: kontakt.priceaicloud@gmail.com.

3. Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

We process data on the basis of: (a) performance of the contract to provide the Service (Art. 6(1)(b)) - account and integration data; (b) our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) - preventing abuse, security and technical diagnostics; (c) legal obligations, for example accounting records once we have to keep them; (d) your consent where required.

4. Who we share data with

  • Anthropic (provider of the Claude AI model) - parts of product data (name, price) to generate pricing suggestions.
  • Search and data providers (such as Serper.dev, SerpApi and similar) and your store platform (WooCommerce, Shopify) - as needed to find competitor prices and integrate with your store.
  • Stripe - payment processing and subscription management.
  • Email delivery provider - to send verification codes and service emails.
  • Hosting provider - to run the infrastructure of the Service.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not share it for third-party marketing.

5. International transfers

We are based in the EU and some of the providers above are located in the United States or elsewhere outside the EU/UK. Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA or the UK, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (where the provider is certified) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. After you delete your account, your data is permanently deleted within a reasonable time, except data we must keep by law (for example billing records).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR and UK GDPR you have the right to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to processing, and receive your data in a portable format. You can delete your account and all related data yourself at any time with "Delete account" in the app. For anything else, write to kontakt.priceaicloud@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (UODO), or, if you live in the UK, with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or with your local EU supervisory authority.

8. California and other US state residents

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. If you live in California or another US state with a privacy law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. Write to kontakt.priceaicloud@gmail.com.

9. Security

Passwords are stored as hashes (bcrypt), never as plain text. Store API keys are stored encrypted and used only to provide the Service; they are not shared with third parties other than those listed in section 4 where needed to provide it.

10. Cookies and similar technologies

The Service uses only essential cookies and local storage: a session cookie to keep you logged in, and a small cookie and local-storage entry to remember your chosen language. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

11. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their personal data.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will notify users of material changes by email or in the app. Questions: kontakt.priceaicloud@gmail.com.
